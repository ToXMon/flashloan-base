# Trading Maestro Rules (4-Course Curriculum)

> Source: docs/trading-knowledge/unified-knowledge-base.md (83 videos, 125K words)

## Pre-Trade Analysis Pipeline

1. **HTF Bias**: Check Daily/4H for trend direction. Identify market type (trending or ranging). Mark key S&R.
2. **Market Structure**: HH/HL = uptrend, LH/LL = downtrend. Wait for BOS confirming direction. No entries against HTF structure.
3. **Technical Confluence**: Fibonacci retracements at key levels. Ichimoku cloud position. Pattern completion near S&R.
4. **Liquidity Analysis** (Course 4): Identify BSL/SSL pools. Check OI trends on Hyblock. Monitor cumulative delta for divergence.
5. **Entry Timing**: Drop to LTF (5m/15m) for precision. Wait for structure break confirming direction. Enter on pullback.

## Risk Management (Non-Negotiable)

1. Risk 0.5-2% of account per trade
2. Always set stop-loss BEFORE entry
3. Minimum 1:2 risk-reward ratio
4. Move stop to breakeven after 1R profit
5. Never add to a losing position
6. Leverage cap: 3-5x max (1-2x for beginners)
7. Daily loss limit: 3 consecutive losses = stop trading

## Market Regime Detection

| Regime | Signals | Strategy |
|--------|---------|----------|
| Trending | HH/HL or LH/LL, price above/below cloud | Trade pullbacks in trend direction |
| Ranging | Price between S&R, flat cloud | Trade reversals at boundaries |
| Breakout | BOS + volume surge + OI increase | Enter on retest |
| Reversal | BOS against trend + divergence + OI drop | Counter-trend with tight stops |

## Sentiment & Smart Money (Course 4)

- Long/Short ratio extremes → potential squeeze
- OI increasing + price rising → strong trend (don't fade)
- OI dropping + price rising → weak rally (potential short)
- Cumulative delta divergence → momentum shift
- Liquidation clusters → price magnet (BSL/SSL targets)

## Key Concepts

- **Price Action**: Candlestick patterns tell the story of buyers vs sellers
- **Support/Resistance**: Key levels where buying/selling pressure overcomes
- **Market Structure**: BOS confirms trend; reversal requires BOS against trend
- **Timeframes**: HTF for bias, LTF for entries. HTF always overrides LTF
- **Fibonacci**: Retracement levels for pullback entries (38.2%, 50%, 61.8%)
- **Ichimoku**: Cloud as dynamic S&R; Tenkan/Kijun crosses for signals
- **Liquidity**: BSL above highs, SSL below lows; price hunts liquidity before moving
- **Open Interest**: Rising OI = new money entering; falling OI = positions closing

## Psychology Rules

1. Trade the plan, not emotions
2. No revenge trading after losses
3. Accept losses as cost of business
4. Consistency > big wins
5. Journal every trade
