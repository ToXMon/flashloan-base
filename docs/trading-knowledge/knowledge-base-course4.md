## Key Concepts Glossary

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| Liquidity | Areas in the market where large orders cluster - buy-side (BSL) and sell-side (SSL) liquidity pools. Price tends to move toward these areas to fill large orders. |
| BSL (Buy-Side Liquidity) | Liquidity pool above current price, typically at swing highs. Price moves up to tap BSL before reversing. |
| SSL (Sell-Side Liquidity) | Liquidity pool below current price, typically at swing lows. Price moves down to tap SSL before reversing. |
| Fair Value Gap (FVG) | A 3-candle imbalance where the first candle wick and third candle wick do not overlap, leaving a gap in price action. Indicates institutional activity. |
| Order Block (OB) | The last bearish candle before a strong bullish move (bullish OB) or last bullish candle before a strong bearish move (bearish OB). Institutional entry zones. |
| Break of Structure (BOS) | When price breaks a previous swing high or low, confirming trend direction. Bullish BOS breaks above swing high; bearish BOS breaks below swing low. |
| Change of Character (CHoCH) | A BOS against the current trend, signaling potential trend reversal. More significant than regular BOS. |
| Funding Rate | Periodic payments between long and short traders in perpetual futures. Positive = longs pay shorts (bullish sentiment); Negative = shorts pay longs (bearish sentiment). |
| Open Interest (OI) | Total number of outstanding derivative contracts not yet settled. Rising OI + rising price = bullish confirmation. Rising OI + falling price = bearish confirmation. |
| Cumulative Delta | Running total of buy volume minus sell volume. Shows whether buyers or sellers are dominating. Divergence between price and cumulative delta signals potential reversal. |
| Future Basis | Difference between futures price and spot price. Large positive basis = overleveraged longs; large negative basis = overleveraged shorts. |
| Sentiment Analysis | Evaluating market mood through variables like funding rate, OI, cumulative delta, and future basis to determine who controls the market. |
| Liquidation Levels | Price levels where leveraged positions get force-closed. Clusters of liquidation levels act as liquidity magnets. |
| Kijun Sen | Ichimoku indicator line calculated as (highest high + lowest low)/2 over 26 periods. Acts as dynamic support/resistance and trend indicator. |
| Kumo (Cloud) | Ichimoku cloud formed by Senkou Span A and B. Price above cloud = bullish; below = bearish. Cloud thickness indicates support/resistance strength. |
| Kumo Pockets | Areas within the Ichimoku cloud where price tends to get trapped or find support/resistance. |
| C Clamps | Ichimoku pattern where Chikou Span clamps against price action, signaling strong trend continuation or reversal. |
| Edge to Edge | Ichimoku trading strategy: enter when price breaks into cloud, target the opposite edge. High-probability cloud trade. |
| Hyblock | Crypto analytics platform providing liquidation data, positions heatmap, and sentiment indicators for leveraged trading. |
| Trend Buddy | A tool/indicator that helps identify trend direction and strength for trade alignment. |
| PAL | Pattern Analysis Logic - systematic approach to identifying and trading chart patterns. |
| FSVZO | Fibonacci-based Smart Volume Zone Oscillator - combines Fibonacci levels with volume analysis. |
| Crayons | Visual marking tool for chart analysis and pattern identification. |
| Genie | Multi-factor analysis tool combining several indicators for trade signals. |
| Heuristics | Rule-based decision frameworks for quick market assessment without complex analysis. |

## Agent Trading Rules (Extracted)

Rules extracted from course content for systematic trading:

1. **Identify liquidity before entering trades** - Always know where BSL and SSL pools are before taking a position.
2. **Fair Value Gaps indicate institutional activity** - FVGs are high-probability entry zones. Look for price to return to unfilled FVGs.
3. **Order blocks mark institutional entry points** - Trade in the direction of the order block after confirmation.
4. **BOS confirms trend, CHoCH signals reversal** - Use BOS for trend continuation trades and CHoCH for reversal setups.
5. **Check funding rate for market sentiment** - Extreme positive funding = overleveraged longs (potential short). Extreme negative = overleveraged shorts (potential long).
6. **Open Interest confirms price moves** - Rising OI + rising price = strong bullish trend. Rising OI + falling price = strong bearish trend.
7. **Cumulative Delta divergence = reversal signal** - If price makes new highs but cumulative delta does not, expect reversal.
8. **Future Basis shows leverage positioning** - Large positive basis means crowded long trade. Large negative means crowded short.
9. **Liquidation levels act as magnets** - Price tends to move toward clusters of liquidation levels to fill those orders.
10. **Use multiple timeframe analysis** - Higher timeframe structure determines direction. Lower timeframe entries for precision.
11. **Ichimoku cloud as dynamic S/R** - Price above Kumo = bullish bias. Price below Kumo = bearish bias. Cloud thickness = strength.
12. **Kijun Sen as equilibrium** - Price tends to return to Kijun Sen. Use it as a dynamic support/resistance level.
13. **Edge to Edge cloud trades** - Enter when price enters the cloud, target the opposite edge for high-probability trades.
14. **Combine sentiment with technicals** - Use funding rate, OI, and cumulative delta alongside chart patterns for higher win rate.
15. **Heatmaps show positioning** - Positions heatmap reveals where traders are concentrated. Position against crowded trades when sentiment diverges.
