## Agent Trading Rules

Extracted principles for systematic trading decision-making:

1. Always identify market structure (higher highs/higher lows vs lower highs/lower lows) before entering trades
2. Use multiple timeframes: higher timeframe for direction, lower timeframe for entry precision
3. Confirm breakouts with volume: high volume = valid breakout, low volume = potential fake-out
4. Place stop losses below structure (support for longs, resistance for shorts), not at arbitrary percentages
5. Risk no more than 1-2% of account per trade to survive losing streaks
6. Target minimum 1:2 risk/reward ratio; aim for 1:3 or better when market structure supports it
7. Wait for retest after breakout before entering: don't chase the initial breakout move
8. Use price action confirmations (candlestick patterns, volume) alongside any indicator
9. In consolidation ranges: buy at support, sell at resistance, use tight stops outside the range
10. Fibonacci retracement levels: 0.618 (golden ratio) and 0.786 are the strongest levels for reversals
11. Volume precedes price: declining volume during a move warns of potential reversal
12. Ichimoku: price above cloud = bullish bias, below cloud = bearish bias, inside cloud = indecision
13. Oscillators (RSI, MACD, Stochastic) are secondary tools: use to confirm price action, not as primary signals
14. RSI divergence (price makes new high but RSI doesn't) is a strong reversal signal
15. Classical patterns have measured moves: the height of the pattern projects the target
16. Match trading style to personality and available screen time (scalping needs full attention, position trading needs patience)
17. Financial instrument selection matters: trade instruments with sufficient liquidity and volatility for your strategy
18. Always have an exit plan before entering: know your stop loss, take profit, and time-based exit criteria
19. Journal every trade: entry reason, exit reason, emotions, outcome. Review weekly for patterns
20. When in doubt, stay out. No trade is also a valid position
