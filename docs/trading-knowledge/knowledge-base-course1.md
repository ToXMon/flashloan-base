## Course Overview

This course covers the foundational concepts every crypto trader needs:

1. **Price Action Fundamentals** — Reading candlesticks, understanding buyer/seller psychology, support and resistance
2. **Market Types** — Identifying trending vs rangebound markets and trading each appropriately
3. **Market Structure** — Understanding and identifying market structure shifts for trade entries
4. **Timeframe Analysis** — Using higher and lower timeframes together for confluence
5. **Risk & Profitability** — Position sizing, risk-reward ratios, and achieving consistent profitability

---

## Key Concepts Glossary

| Concept | Definition |
|---------|------------|
| Price Action | The movement of price over time, showing the story of buyers and sellers |
| Candlestick | A chart type showing open, high, low, close for a time period |
| Support | A price level where buying pressure historically overcomes selling |
| Resistance | A price level where selling pressure historically overcomes buying |
| Trend | A sustained directional move in price (up = higher highs/lows, down = lower highs/lows) |
| Range | Price moving sideways between support and resistance levels |
| Market Structure | The pattern of highs and lows that defines the current market direction |
| HH/HL | Higher High / Higher Low — indicates uptrend structure |
| LH/LL | Lower High / Lower Low — indicates downtrend structure |
| BOS | Break of Structure — when price breaks a previous swing point, signaling trend continuation or reversal |
| HTF | Higher Timeframe (e.g., 4H, 1D) — used for directional bias |
| LTF | Lower Timeframe (e.g., 5m, 15m) — used for precise entries |
| Risk-Reward Ratio | The ratio of potential profit to potential loss on a trade |
| Position Size | The amount of capital allocated to a single trade |
| Drawdown | The peak-to-trough decline in account equity |

## Agent Trading Rules (Extracted from Course 1)

These rules should guide the trading agent's decision-making:

### Entry Rules
1. Always identify the market type (trending or range) before looking for entries
2. In a trend, trade pullbacks to key levels; in a range, trade reversals at boundaries
3. Use HTF for directional bias, LTF for entry precision
4. Wait for market structure confirmation before entering
5. Never enter against the higher timeframe trend

### Risk Management Rules
1. Risk no more than 1-2% of account per trade
2. Always set stop-loss before entering
3. Target minimum 1:2 risk-reward ratio
4. Track win rate to ensure edge remains positive
5. Reduce position size during losing streaks

### Market Structure Rules
1. Uptrend = HH + HL; look for longs at HL tests
2. Downtrend = LH + LL; look for shorts at LH tests  
3. Break of Structure (BOS) confirms trend continuation
4. Trend reversal requires a BOS against the prevailing direction
5. Range = price between clear support and resistance; no trend

### Timeframe Hierarchy
1. Start with daily/4H for overall direction
2. Drop to 1H for zone identification
3. Use 5m/15m for entry timing
4. Never trade the 1m timeframe as primary
5. Higher timeframe bias always overrides lower timeframe signals
